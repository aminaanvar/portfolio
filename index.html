How I work

My approach

Confident users who don't need someone beside them at every step.

Start with the learner

Talk to subject-matter experts, check participant data, and map what each group actually needs to do.

Keep it consistent

Clear templates and navigation so that once people learn one course, they know them all.

Build for self-service

Short guides, how-to videos and interactive content people can come back to, with live training where it adds most.

Measure and improve

Use surveys, completion data and recurring questions to decide what to change next. AI helps with first drafts; I review everything.
